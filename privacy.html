Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

This privacy policy describes how Elaich OÜ ("we", "us") handles personal data in connection with this website, elaich.com. We keep this simple, because the website itself is simple.

Who we are

Elaich OÜ is a private limited company registered in Estonia (registry code 16949657, VAT number EE102731882), with its registered address at Sepapaja tn 6, 15551 Tallinn, Estonia. For anything related to this policy, contact us at marouane@elaich.com.

What we collect on this website

Nothing, actively. This website:

  • does not use cookies;
  • does not run analytics or tracking scripts;
  • does not include advertising;
  • has no user accounts and no forms that store data.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (operated by GitHub, Inc.), which may log standard technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs for security and operational purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement for details. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which involves your browser requesting font files from Google's servers; see Google's privacy policy.

If you contact us

If you email or call us, we receive the personal data you choose to share (such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message). We use it solely to respond to you and to conduct the business relationship you initiate. We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for marketing.

Client work

Data we process while performing engineering work for clients is handled under the contract governing that engagement, not under this policy.

Your rights

As an Estonian company, we are subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Estonia, the Data Protection Inspectorate, aki.ee). To exercise any of these rights, email marouane@elaich.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
